# Support Policy: Decision Notes

**Status:** decision recorded, wording and pricing not yet chosen.
**Recorded:** 23 October 2026.

## The decision

Hands-on support for **free events** will no longer be free. Answering organizer questions takes executive time. The organizer FAQ (`docs/FAQ_ORGANIZERS.md`) is the free, self-serve layer and should be kept exhaustive. Anything beyond it attracts a fee.

## Guard rails (recommended, to protect trust)

These should stay free whatever an organizer pays. Charging for them would mean charging people for Orkora's own faults, and would damage trust quickly.

1. **Platform faults:** anything not working as the FAQ describes, including outages, failed emails caused by the platform, and bugs.
2. **Account access and security:** locked out, compromised account, wrong organization.
3. **Money problems:** a buyer charged without a ticket, settlement holds, refund failures.

## What could be charged

- Setting up an event on the organizer's behalf (tiers, questions, Story Mode, branding).
- Writing or configuring email campaigns for them.
- Bulk data work: corrections, merges, imports, cleanup of duplicates the organizer created.
- Re-sending tickets in bulk. There is no organizer-facing resend button today; the resend tool is internal.
- On-the-day standby or remote assistance during an event.
- Training sessions for the organizer's team.

## Positioning options (to decide later)

| Option | How it works | Fits when |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Per-request fee** | A flat fee per support request outside the FAQ and the guard rails | Requests are occasional and small |
| **B. Event Assist package** | A one-off fee per event covering setup help and day-of standby | Larger events that want reassurance |
| **C. Support included with paid events** | Free events get FAQ and email only; paid events (once the platform fee is live) include standard support | Ties support cost to platform revenue, and nudges organizers towards paid ticketing |
| **D. Monthly support plan** | A subscription for priority response | Repeat organizers running several events a month |

Options can be combined, for example C as the default with B available on request.

## Wording principles

- Lead with what is free and self-serve, then present paid help as an optional service, not a penalty.
- Name response times for each level, so the fee buys something concrete.
- Never ask an organizer to pay before confirming the issue is not a platform fault.

## Before announcing

1. Choose the option or options, the prices and the response times.
2. Decide how payment is taken: invoice, or a Paystack payment link.
3. Update the "Getting help" section of the FAQ. It currently says paid help exists and that pricing is to be published.
4. Terms: as of 23 October 2026, `apps/web/legal/terms.md` only lists hello@orkora.events as the support contact, and `organizer.md` makes no support promise, so no free-support commitment needs withdrawing. Adding a paid support clause is still a material change; the Terms require 30 days' notice for those. Ask counsel.
5. Publish the FAQ on the site, so the free layer exists before the paid one is announced.
