# Orkora for Event Brands

**Published:** 8 July 2026
**Author:** The Orkora Team

The category has moved. Event brands stopped selling tickets a while ago. They now sell social identity, belonging, atmosphere, and worlds people want to be seen inside of. The strongest ones are not the ones with the biggest lineups. They are the ones with the clearest identity, the strongest atmosphere, and the most intentional storytelling.

Every existing tool for organisers was built for the old model. Register, checkout, ticket, done. That model does not describe what event brands actually do anymore.

Today we are releasing Orkora for Event Brands.

## What we are shipping

**Brand home.** Every organiser now has a persistent public presence at `orkora.events/o/your-brand`. Not a login-gated dashboard. A public home for your brand that lives year-round. Upcoming events, past editions, the cast of collaborators, the house style. Attendees who arrive for one event discover your whole world.

**Story mode.** Your public event page stops being a checkout form and becomes a designed narrative. Choose one of four templates. Compose from blocks: hero video, artist and creator features, moodboard, playlist, brand collabs, ticket tiers. It looks like editorial content, not a lineup poster.

**Shareable moments.** Every ticket purchase now auto-generates a personalised card in your brand style. Story format, feed format, WhatsApp friendly. Your attendees share themselves into your marketing. Their friends see it, click through, buy in. The growth loop that every event brand runs manually is now built into the platform.

That is what is live today. There is more shipping soon.

## What is coming next

Ambassador programme. Invite creators as first-class collaborators. Tracked referral links, comp tickets, activity dashboard, optional revenue share. Co-marketing without spreadsheets.

Campaigns as series. The email tool that ships with Orkora already lets you send a broadcast. Series turns it into a sequenced arc: teaser two weeks out, lookbook one week out, playlist reveal three days before, day-of "what to expect". Templated. Ship the story, not one blast at a time.

On-day content capture. During the event, your photographer, creators, and attendees upload directly. Files land in your archive tagged by session, moderated by your team, surfacing to your brand home in real time. Post-event, your archive lives forever.

Content-first analytics. Ticket revenue is one measure. Shares, UGC volume, hashtag reach, community growth are the others. Both live on the dashboard. Both count.

## Why we built this

Because our organisers were already doing it manually.

The event brands worth watching right now think in three shifts: identity over lineup, audience over attendance, compounding over one-off. Their marketing team runs their Instagram like a magazine, drops shareable graphics for every announcement, invites creators to be part of the story, and stitches together photo assets from three different photographers into a recap the week after. Then they do it all again for the next edition.

None of that lived in a single tool. Some of it lived in Canva. Some in Later. Some in a Google Drive folder that nobody could find. The ticketing platform they used was the last thing they touched, days before the event.

We looked at that reality and realised the ticketing platform should be the first thing, not the last. It should hold the brand's public presence, its audience, its content, its momentum. Everything else in the event brand's stack should feed off it.

That is what Orkora for Event Brands is.

## Who this is for

If your event has a hashtag before it has a poster, you are our organiser.

Specifically:
- Aesthetic culture nights where the door is the door and the crowd is the point.
- Diaspora festivals where the audience spans four continents and three currencies.
- Youth identity brands that live half online and half in the room.
- Curated dinners and members-only sessions where the seat is a signal.
- Cultural runs where the same brand throws four editions a year and each one has to outdraw the last.

This is not for free meetups. Not for corporate seminars. Not for compliance training. There are perfectly good tools for those; we are not those tools.

## What stays the same

Every ticketing thing you already trusted us for stays exactly the same and gets better.

Multi-currency in one event across NGN, GHS, KES, USD. Payment providers routed automatically per buyer. Verify-on-return settlement so a paid ticket lands in ten minutes, not an hour. Refunds that self-serve. QR check-in that reads offline at the door. Live chat, polls, Q&A during sessions. All of it, still there, still working.

If you were on the platform yesterday, you have story mode, brand home, and shareable ticket cards today. No migration. No re-onboarding. Nothing broken.
