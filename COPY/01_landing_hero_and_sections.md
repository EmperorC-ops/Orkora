## Section 1: The category has moved

### Events used to be transactions. Now they are worlds.

The events getting talked about online are not the ones with the biggest lineups. They are the ones with the clearest identity, the strongest atmosphere, and the most intentional storytelling. Audiences do not just remember what happened at the event. They remember how being there made them feel, and how being associated with it made them look online.

The event has become a brand. The brand has an audience. The audience follows the brand from one event to the next, from the pre-drop to the after-party, from the moodboard to the recap film.

Every existing event platform treats an event as a discrete transaction. Orkora is built for how events actually work now.

## Section 2: The three shifts we build for

### Identity over lineup

Your public event page is not a form. It is your world's front door. Story mode, cinematic hero, aesthetic gallery, cast of collaborators, brand collab callouts. Attendees who land there feel the culture before they see the tiers.

### Audience over attendance

Every ticket sold is a person you now know. Their profile, their taste, their share graph, their history with your brand. Not a receipt. A member.

### Compounding over one-off

The old model resets after each event. The new model compounds. Your brand home stays live year-round. Past editions become the marketing for the next one. The audience carries forward. Every event is heavier than the last.

## Section 3: What Orkora gives you

### A brand home that lives year-round

Your organisation gets a persistent public presence at `orkora.events/o/your-brand`. Upcoming events, past highlights, cast of collaborators, house style. New attendees discover the whole brand from any single event page.

### An event page that reads like an editorial

Not a checkout form. A scroll-narrative you compose from templated blocks: hero video, artist and creator features, moodboard, "what to wear", playlist, brand collabs, ticket tiers. Four presets to start from: Editorial, Cinematic, Underground, Runway.

### Shareable moments built into every ticket

Every purchase auto-generates a personalised card in your brand style. Story format, feed format, WhatsApp-friendly format. Your attendees share themselves into your marketing.

### Content series in campaigns

Extend the email campaigns you already have into a series: teaser two weeks out, lookbook one week out, playlist reveal three days before, day-of "what to expect". Templated. Ship the story arc, not one blast at a time.

### Creator collaborations as first-class

Invite creators as ambassadors. Each gets a unique referral link, a tracked activity dashboard, comp tickets, and a share of the revenue if you offer one. You get co-marketing without spreadsheets.

### On-the-day content capture

Your photographer, your creators, your attendees, uploading during the event through the mobile app or a private link. Tagged by session, moderated by your team, surfacing to the brand home in real time. Post-event, the archive lives forever.

### Live engagement that turns into content

Chat, polls, Q&A during the event. The conversations, the calls-outs, the moments. All searchable in the archive. All feedstock for the recap.

### Ticket infrastructure that just works

Multi-currency in one event: USD, NGN, GHS, KES. Provider-routed automatically per buyer. Verify-on-return settlement so a paid ticket lands in ten minutes, not an hour. Refunds that self-serve. QR check-in that reads offline at the door.

## Section 5: How it feels to use

### Day one

You sign up in incognito. You claim your brand's URL. You upload your logo, choose your colours, drop your bio. Your brand home is live inside three minutes.

### Day seven

You have an event on the calendar. Story mode is composed, tiers are set, the confirmation email is styled in your brand. You share the URL. Tickets start moving. The first buyer gets a shareable card that lands in a group chat you cannot see. Someone in that chat buys next.

### Day of

Your staff open the scanner on their phone. The door line moves. Attendance ticks up on the dashboard. Your photographer uploads. Chat lights up during the headline set. Your ambassador drops a story clip.

### Day after

The archive is already assembled. Photos tagged by set. Best moments queued for the recap post. Attendee CSV exported for the sponsor report. Refund reconciliation runs on its own. You start composing the story for the next edition.
