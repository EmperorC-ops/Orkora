**Length:** designed to fit in a mobile preview pane. Under 250 words.

## Notes on outreach

**Sequencing.** Send Version A. If no reply in five business days, send a two-line follow-up ("still keen to explore this, happy to send a 90-second video walkthrough if easier"). If no reply after another five days, park.

**Personalisation.** The [SPECIFIC RECENT MOMENT] line is the whole pitch. Do not send without it. Skim their last three Instagram posts before writing.

**Volume.** Send no more than five of these a week. Every launch partner is a real relationship. Automate the calendar, never the pitch.

**Follow-up.** After the call, send Version C within 24 hours. After the partnership is confirmed, agree the case-study terms in the same thread. The case study is the marketing asset that lets us send Version A to the next ten brands.

**What we offer them.** Six months of everything free (platform fee waiver, priority support, custom feature build, joint marketing). What we ask in return: they migrate their next edition onto Orkora, they run it publicly on the platform, they let us publish the case study with data and quotes.

**Kill criteria.** If the brand's response to "we would build any missing piece specific to your workflow" is "we do not have missing pieces, our current setup works" — pass. Launch partners are chosen for the leverage of their story, not for how much they need us. If they do not need us, the case study writes itself but the migration will not happen.
