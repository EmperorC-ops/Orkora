# Platform Fee Notice to Organizers (DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW)

Status: draft. This is a starting point for the 60-day written notice required by
Terms of Service sections 4 and 7 before a platform fee can be introduced. It is
not legal text. Counsel should review and finalize wording, and confirm it
satisfies the notice obligation in the Terms. Placeholders in ALL CAPS are filled
in at send time.

Suggested delivery: email to every organizer account, from a monitored address
(for example billing@orkora.events), with the send date recorded so the effective
date can be set at least 60 calendar days later.

---

Subject: A platform fee is coming to Orkora on [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

Hi [ORGANIZER_NAME],

Thank you for building your events on Orkora during our beta, when we charged no
platform fee on ticket sales.

We are writing to give you advance notice, as our Terms require, that Orkora will
begin charging a platform fee on paid tickets starting [EFFECTIVE_DATE], which is
at least 60 days from the date of this notice.

What the fee is

- 3% of the ticket price plus a flat fee per paid ticket. For tickets sold in
  Nigerian Naira, the flat fee is NGN 1,350 per ticket. For tickets sold in US
  Dollars, it is USD 0.99 per ticket. Other currencies will be listed before they
  are enabled.
- The fee applies only to paid tickets. Free tickets remain free.
- Your payment provider's own processing fees are unchanged and separate from
  this fee. Orkora does not collect those.

What stays the same for your current events

- Every event you created before [EFFECTIVE_DATE] keeps its current fee
  arrangement, which is no platform fee, until that event ends. The fee applies
  only to events you create on or after [EFFECTIVE_DATE].

Your choice: who pays the fee

- For each event, you choose whether you absorb the fee or pass it on to
  attendees. If you absorb it, the fee comes out of your proceeds and attendees
  pay only the ticket price. If you pass it on, the fee is added to what
  attendees pay and is shown to them clearly before checkout, and you receive the
  full ticket price. You set this per event under Service fee in the event
  editor.

Getting paid

- To sell paid tickets once the fee is live, connect a payout account under
  Settings, Payments. Funds settle directly to your connected account, and the
  platform fee is taken as a split. Orkora does not hold your funds.

Refunds

- If you refund an order in full, the attendee is refunded everything they paid,
  and the platform fee is returned as well, so Orkora keeps nothing on a refunded
  order.

The updated Terms and fee details are at [TERMS_URL]. If you have questions, reply
to this email or contact us at [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

Thank you for being part of Orkora.

The Orkora team
